Abstract

Five Minutes to Show: Risk Management in Live AV Production

Five Minutes to Show is an interactive story created in Twine that explores the challenges of managing audiovisual technology during a live event. The player takes on the role of a lead AV technician dealing with equipment failures, troubleshooting, and unexpected problems during an awards ceremony. Each decision affects how the event plays out and shows the importance of teamwork, communication, and quick thinking. The project is inspired by my experience working in live AV production. It also connects to research by Liu-Lastres and Cahyanto (2023), which discusses how event professionals prepare for and respond to unexpected problems. My goal is to give readers a better understanding of what happens behind the scenes and show that successful events depend on more than just technical knowledge.

Works Cited

Liu-Lastres, Bingjie, and I. Putu Cahyanto. "Are We Always Ready? Examining Event Professionals' Approaches to Risk and Crisis Management and Resilience." Tourism Management Perspectives, vol. 46, 2023, article 101073. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tmp.2023.101073 .
