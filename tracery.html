Loudspeakers have changed significantly over the past century as new materials and technologies improved how sound is reproduced. Developments such as moving-coil drivers, crossover networks, stronger magnets, and digital signal processing have made speakers more accurate, efficient, and adaptable. This project uses Tracery to explore how these changes shaped loudspeaker design and its use in homes, classrooms, theaters, and professional audio systems (Rice and Kellogg). Rice, Chester W., and Edward W. Kellogg. “Notes on the Development of a New Type of Hornless Loudspeaker.” Journal of the Audio Engineering Society, vol. 30, no. 7/8, 1982, pp. 512–521.
